XBTorch Documentation

XBTorch is a research framework for optimization and study of memristive neural networks based on emerging neuromorphic systems. It provides a modular and extensible set of tools for modeling device non-idealities, mapping neural networks to crossbar accelerators, exploring fault-tolerant architectures, etc.

In particular, XBTorch streamlines:

  • Hardware-aware decompositions and hardware-accurate training

  • Weight encoding schemes with realistic device modeling

  • Deployment strategies for inference across noisy and defective accelerators

  • Fault-tolerant and analog noise-tolerant algorithm design
